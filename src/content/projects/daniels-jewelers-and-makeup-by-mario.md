## Context

Two established Shopify Plus stores maintained by Vaangroup, both featured in the agency's own portfolio. I was one of the developers on each, and my part was a set of focused features and fixes that shoppers can see on the live stores.

## Daniel's Jewelers

- **Gift with purchase in the cart.** Moved the promotion to a single tier where the customer chooses their gift, with a locked view until the threshold is met, a reminder overlay when a shopper checks out without claiming it, settings to configure it, and a limit of one gift per order.
- **Cart and product pages.** A donation section in the cart, gallery actions and a text-over-image layout on product pages.
- **Accessibility.** A mega menu that works for keyboard-only users, filters grouped in fieldsets for screen readers, and alt text and ARIA roles on video posters.

## Makeup by Mario

- **Performance.** Sliders that only start when they enter the viewport, a non-blocking font loader, third-party scripts deferred, legacy duplicates removed and image optimization.
- **Consent.** Manual blocking of tracking scripts until the visitor consents, for GDPR.
- **Subscriptions and product pages.** An account-page update for subscriptions and a fix for the back-in-stock "notify me" button.
